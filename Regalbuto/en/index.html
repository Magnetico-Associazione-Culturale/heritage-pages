A town on the old wheat road

Regalbuto stands on a hill between Catania and Enna, on the old road between Catania and Palermo. Rebuilt in the 17th and 18th centuries along the strada Maestra, it looks down on Lake Pozzillo. These are some of the places you will find in the app.

  • Mother Church of San Basilio

    On piazza della Repubblica, with a bell tower about 50 metres tall.

  • Santa Maria la Croce

    The Baroque façade, completed in 1774, also in a 360° view.

  • Hermitage of Sant'Antonio Abate

    On the hill outside town, with its settlement carved into the rock.

  • San Calogero rock

    The ruins of the town's first church and a 360° panorama.
